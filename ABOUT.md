# About Kerman Haynes

Kerman Haynes is an inventor, systems architect, author and founder of AetherForge Labs, Inc. He is the Original Architect and Project Founder of AI-Continuity.

His work connects the design of complex systems with the people whose judgment gives those systems purpose. [AUTHORS.md](AUTHORS.md) contains his full first-person account of AI-Continuity, AetherForge and the philosophy behind his work.

[AetherForge](https://afcopilot.com) is an AI-powered invention operating system designed first for neurodivergent inventors, while welcoming all inventors. Its background structure preserves documentation, evidence, provenance and rigor while inventors explore and develop work in their own order. [ORIGIN.md](ORIGIN.md) explains how that work informed the independent AI-Continuity project.

## Winner Loses All

In *Winner Loses All*, Kerman presents what he believes to be the underlying architecture supporting a functioning civilization. Its central principle is the living authorship of human judgment: accumulated knowledge and stored judgment serve people, while living human judgment remains necessary as conditions change.

The book invites readers to examine that architecture and judge for themselves. It does not ask them to abandon expertise, institutions or technology. Readers retain authorship of their conclusions.

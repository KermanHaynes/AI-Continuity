# Community conduct

Treat participants with respect. Discuss ideas, evidence and technical choices directly. Make room for different backgrounds, communication styles and ways of thinking.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing and deliberate disclosure of private information are unacceptable in project-managed spaces.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation in proportion to the conduct. Where practical, they should explain the action and allow an appeal to an uninvolved designated maintainer.

For a conduct concern, email [winnerlosesall@gmail.com](mailto:winnerlosesall@gmail.com), identify AI-Continuity and request a private follow-up. Keep the initial message free of sensitive allegations or personal details; agree on a suitable channel before sharing them. GitHub’s platform reporting tools may also be used for platform abuse. Reports should be handled with discretion and shared only as needed to address the concern.

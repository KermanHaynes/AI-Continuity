# Contributing to AI-Continuity

Bug fixes, documentation improvements, model/provider integrations, cross-model interoperability, installation and usability improvements, accessibility, new applications, testing and reliability improvements, and well-explained architectural proposals are welcome.

You may read, copy, download, fork, modify, use and redistribute AI-Continuity under Apache License 2.0. These freedoms apply to your own copies. No outside contribution enters the official distribution without authorized review and approval under Kerman Haynes’s founder-led stewardship.

AI-assisted contributions are welcome. Contributors remain responsible for understanding proposed changes, explaining their purpose, verifying correctness and providing applicable evidence or tests. The use of AI assistance is not a reason to reject a contribution.

## Propose a change

For a substantial change, open an issue describing the problem, the intended behavior and the relevant evidence. Explain any effect on source preservation, stable identities, current knowledge, history, work records or compatibility. Smaller fixes may be submitted directly for review.

Preserve the architecture’s authority boundaries. Original evidence remains original; reviewed knowledge identifies established positions and their development; operational records retain commitments and outcomes. Changes should retain source references, historical reasoning and the distinction between accepted decisions and proposals.

## Prepare a contribution

Use synthetic inputs and minimal reproductions. Include the relevant validation or test results and explain the behavior before and after the change. Run the checks described in [VALIDATION.md](docs/VALIDATION.md). Preserve existing tests and add meaningful coverage for changed behavior.

Do not submit credentials, private conversations, customer material, personal records or material you lack permission to disclose. Identify third-party sources and their licenses. Review every contribution for correctness, provenance and the right to submit it.

## Submission terms and credit

Contributions intentionally submitted for inclusion are offered under Apache License 2.0 unless explicitly stated otherwise before submission. You must have the right to contribute the material under those terms. This contribution process does not assign your copyright to AetherForge Labs. A separate agreement is needed for an ownership transfer or different license terms.

No mandatory CLA or DCO sign-off process is established by this policy. Contributors are credited for their work using their approved public identity. The project retains its original architectural attribution while recognizing subsequent contributions.

Maintainers review changes for usefulness, correctness and consistency with the architecture. They may request revisions or decline a change with an explanation where practical. See [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md), [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Follow [software maintenance](docs/MAINTENANCE.md) for regression scenarios, schema/interface compatibility, source preservation, transaction recovery and the change verification sequence.

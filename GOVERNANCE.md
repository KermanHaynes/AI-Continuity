# Founder-led governance

Kerman Haynes is the Original Architect and Project Founder. The official [AI-Continuity repository](https://github.com/KermanHaynes/AI-Continuity) is hosted under his personal GitHub account, KermanHaynes. AetherForge Labs, Inc. is the Copyright Holder and Project Steward.

## Official project decisions

Kerman retains authority over official project direction, architectural changes, reviewed contributions, merge approval and releases. He may expressly delegate a defined maintenance task; delegation does not transfer project ownership or general decision-making authority.

Members of the public may submit suggestions, issues and pull requests. No outside contribution is accepted into the official distribution without authorized review and approval. A submitted proposal does not itself change the official project. Major architectural proposals should explain their purpose, reasoning, compatibility effects and consequences for evidence preservation and user authority before substantial implementation begins.

## Open-source freedom

Anyone may read, clone, download, fork, modify, use and redistribute the software under Apache License 2.0. People control their own forks. Founder stewardship governs what enters the official distribution and does not reduce permissions granted by the license.

## Participation and support

Contributors receive credit for their work and follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Discuss ideas, evidence and technical choices respectfully. AI-assisted contributions follow the same responsibility and review standards.

Sponsorship and professional engagements do not purchase decision-making authority, preferential acceptance, merge rights or control of releases. No foundation, board, committee or sponsor-controlled governance structure is established.

## Releases

Official release decisions remain with Kerman or a maintainer expressly authorized for that release. Release notes record material changes, compatibility effects and relevant operational requirements. Governance changes do not revoke rights already granted under Apache 2.0.

# Complete topic and project reconstruction

Reconstruction preserves intellectual development, not just a current answer. Its durable lineage is:

**Original SOT → Complete Evolution Record → Draft Evolution Narrative → Approved Final Evolution Narrative → Authoritative Current Knowledge**

Use the [reconstruction prompt](../prompts/03-reconstruct-topic.md) after importing authentic source evidence.

## Define the scope

Record the topic/project identity and boundaries, included providers and archives, known historical start date, optional end date, source inventory version and any unavailable material. If the start is unknown, begin with the earliest available records. Include every available conversation intersecting the selected period and the full dialogue of each conversation crossing a boundary. Unknown or ambiguous times cannot justify silent exclusion.

Freeze a reading census of all available conversations in that scope with IDs, source versions, transcript hashes and locations. Conversation titles, topic maps, keywords, semantic search and estimated relevance do not choose the reading set. One paragraph in an apparently unrelated conversation may carry a decisive development.

If sources change, retain the previous census and completed coverage. Compare identities and versions, then explicitly add or re-read affected content. New messages or corrected bytes need review; unchanged fully read evidence need not be processed again.

## Choose depth before reading

- **Detailed:** extensive reasoning, historical development, WHY behind decisions, alternatives, revisions and intellectual relationships.
- **Standard:** meaningful evolution, important reasoning, decisions, corrections and material changes.
- **Concise:** major developments, established positions and essential reasoning with links to deeper evidence.

Depth controls the detail of derived artifacts. Every option still requires complete reading of every scoped conversation. Establish it before reading so relevant reasoning is captured at the requested detail rather than lost through early compression.

## Read exhaustively

Read the entire authentic user-assistant dialogue of every available conversation in the census across every provider included. Read all available native branches represented by those conversations rather than treating the last branch as the whole history. Search and indexes may navigate or locate records; they never substitute for the full read or exclude conversations.

When context or time is limited, use bounded sequential reading within conversations and persistent checkpoints. Record exactly which stable messages or source ranges have been read. Do not label a conversation fully read until all its available dialogue and branches in scope have been examined. A checkpoint is coverage evidence, not a relevance filter.

Retain observations, initial ideas, conceptual developments, reasoning and WHY, circumstances of decisions, alternatives, rejections and reasons, deferred and superseded positions, corrections, experiments, outcomes, shifting significance, related topics, important assistant contributions, user reactions and unresolved questions. Silence does not establish agreement or rejection. Link developments to exact source/message references and preserve their original chronology.

## Save the complete evolution before drafting

Save working notes during reading. When coverage is complete, assemble and durably save the complete evolution record with source scope, version, evidence links and outstanding source gaps. Verify its file/hash and local commit; publish checkpoints to the user’s private remote within granted authority when possible. A local-only checkpoint is retained but not called remotely preserved.

A partially examined census does not support a completed reconstruction. If material is inaccessible or incomplete, record it explicitly, continue independent available work, and obtain recovery or an explicit revised scope. A final narrative may describe the available revised scope without pretending the original requested scope was fully examined.

Only after the complete evolution record is saved does integrated narrative drafting begin. If that later stage is interrupted, resume from the preserved record, not from a new full historical read.

## Draft, review and establish current knowledge

Save an independent versioned draft narrative. Review it with the user, retaining each prior draft and its corrections. An approved final narrative is a distinct versioned artifact with approval evidence/date, source scope and parent artifact references. Later developments do not silently rewrite it.

Separately establish CURRENT in the user’s preferred form and level of detail. It can include the full approved narrative, a concise view, topical/subtopical structure, chronology, an outline or a hybrid. Even if the user chooses to reproduce the whole narrative in CURRENT, retain the final narrative as an independently versioned historical artifact. CURRENT links to the evolution, drafts and final narrative for deeper retrieval.

Use [artifact and checkpoint conventions](RECONSTRUCTION_ARTIFACTS.md), [review prompt](../prompts/04-review-narrative.md), and [Git operating conventions](GIT_OPERATIONS.md). Once established, the topic enters normal session-based continuity and direct maintenance; a changed position does not require a new exhaustive reconstruction by default.
